Legal Obligations Reminder
A liquidator must file with the registrar a notice of any change to the information shown in the Corporate Register. The most common information you are legally responsible to update includes:
Liquidation Reports - file a liquidation report each year within two months after each anniversary of the date on which the company was recognized. This ensures your company remains in good standing with the Registrar.
Company Address Changes - update any changes to a company's registered or records office addresses as they occur.
